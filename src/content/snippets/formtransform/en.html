FormTransform is a web app that converts surveys between the standards of the CDL survey ecosystem: XLSForm questionnaires (e.g. from KoboToolbox) to the LimeSurvey TSV format, and XLSForm or LimeSurvey TSV to DDI Codebook 2.5 metadata. Together with a KoboToolbox data export, it also produces a response dataset matching the DDI description. Everything runs in the browser; no data leaves your device. It is based on the JavaScript library formtransform.
