formtransform is the TypeScript library (with the formtransform command-line tool) that the FormTransform app and qwacback are built on. It converts XLSForm to the LimeSurvey TSV format and to DDI Codebook 2.5 (together with a data export, also to a matching response dataset), LimeSurvey TSV to DDI and to XLSForm, and DDI back to XLSForm. Everything it exports also runs in the browser. A type registry defines which XLSForm features it supports; anything not registered is rejected. See the supported XLSForm subset.
